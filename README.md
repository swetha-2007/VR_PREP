
An AI-powered Virtual Reality mock interview platform designed to provide realistic, personalized interview practice. Users can prepare for company- and role-specific interviews, interact with an AI interviewer, practice adaptive follow-up questions, and receive detailed feedback on their performance after each session.

The project follows a Design Thinking approach, focusing on understanding students' interview challenges, identifying gaps in existing preparation methods, and designing a more realistic and accessible interview-practice experience.

Future scope: AI-assisted real interviews for companies and virtual group discussion simulations.
